Steps

  • Sort edges by weight.
  • Keep an edge when it joins two different components.
  • Stop once the tree contains V - 1 edges.

Complexity & Applications

Complexity

  • Time: O(E log E)
  • Space: O(V + E)
  • Effective for sparse and dense weighted graphs

Applications

  • Infrastructure planning
  • Minimum cost wiring
  • Network backbone design

Advantages & Disadvantages

Advantages

  • Simple and intuitive for MST construction.
  • Works well with edge sorting and cycle checks.

Disadvantages

  • Needs careful cycle detection.
  • Less direct than Prim for some implementations.
