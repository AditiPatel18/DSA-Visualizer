Steps

  • Start from a seed node.
  • Choose the smallest edge that reaches a new node.
  • Repeat until the tree spans the graph.

Complexity & Applications

Complexity

  • Time: O(E log V)
  • Space: O(V)
  • Ideal for dense or sparse weighted graphs

Applications

  • Network design
  • Cluster connections
  • Cost minimization

Advantages & Disadvantages

Advantages

  • Produces a low-cost connected network.
  • Works efficiently with priority queues.

Disadvantages

  • Needs a connected graph.
  • Not appropriate for directed graphs.
